<translation>Victory of Heracles and Hauronas</translation>

The inscriptions appear in relief on a lead sling bullet dated to the second century BCE. Side A contains the word for "victory" while Side B contains the names of two deities - Heracles and Hauronas - in the genitive case, indicating that the victory belongs to or is granted by these gods who were the patron deities of ancient Iamnia.
