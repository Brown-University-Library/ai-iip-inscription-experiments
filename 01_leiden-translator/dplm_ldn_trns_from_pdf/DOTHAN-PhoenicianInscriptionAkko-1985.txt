<translation>
By order: to the guild of (metal?) artisans; they shall give a valuable basin to שלת, the overseer of the shrine(s); (likewise they shall give) metal cups (golden?) 10, and vessels with inlay (round ringstands?) 25, and large pyxides 70, and pots (on stands) 60, and decanters 60, and smiths' blown vessels 57 and small deep bowls 30.
